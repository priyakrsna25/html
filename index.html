Pawan Kalyan

Pawan Kalyan is an Indian actor and politician who primarily works in Telugu cinema.

Pawan Kalyan was born on 2 September 1971 in Andhra Pradesh, India. He entered the film industry and became popular for his unique acting style and screen presence.

Film Career

Pawan Kalyan made his acting debut with the Telugu film Akkada Ammayi Ikkada Abbayi in 1996. He later appeared in many successful films.

Some of his popular movies include Tholi Prema, Jalsa, Gabbar Singh, and Attarintiki Daredi.

Political Career

Pawan Kalyan founded the Jana Sena Party in 2014. He entered active politics and worked on various public and social issues.

His political journey has attracted a large number of supporters and followers.

Achievements

Pawan Kalyan has received several awards for his contribution to Telugu cinema. He is also known for his involvement in social and political activities.

Personal Life

Pawan Kalyan is known for maintaining a strong connection with his fans and supporters. He continues to work in both cinema and public life.
